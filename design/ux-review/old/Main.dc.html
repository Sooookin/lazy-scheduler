LazyScheduler · UX review · PC + Android

Make every everyday action one tap —
and every tap undoable.

The app already looks calm and finished. What holds it back is how many steps the common jobs take, and how little room there is for a mistake. This review ranks every issue by how much it gets in the way of daily use. The numbered orange markers on the other boards point to the same numbers below.

Rules for the redesign

Fast for the frequent

Checking off, adding, and seeing what's next happen dozens of times a day. Each should take one action.

Nothing final

Complete, delete and skip always offer "되돌리기" (undo) for a few seconds. No confirm dialogs needed.

Say it once

Each fact appears in one place. Big type goes to what the user acts on, not to decoration.

Same words, both apps

할 일 · 반복 · 메모 everywhere: in tabs, forms, cards and notifications.

Findings ranked by how much they get in the way

1
There is no undo P0
PC home · phone lists · notification cards

One click completes, and the row jumps to the bottom. On the phone a tap anywhere on the row completes. Delete asks for confirmation instead of being reversible, which slows down the safe case.

Fix

A 5-second toast (PC) / snackbar (phone) after complete, delete and skip: “‘국내 일임 매매’ 완료 · 되돌리기”. Drop the delete confirm dialog.

2
Adding takes a whole form P0
PC 새 항목 · phone ＋ 새 항목

Nine fields over three sections, and the first tab is “반복되는 일” (recurring), the rarest thing to add. A quick deadline needs a tab switch, a date and a time typed in a native time box (“-- --:--”).

Fix

An add bar that is always visible. Type naturally, e.g. “내일 3시 보고서”, “매월 둘째 영업일 월간 보고”, “금요일까지 예산안”. The app shows what it understood as chips and Enter saves. ⇥ or “자세히” opens the full editor, pre-filled.

3
Tap means “done”, and editing is hidden P0
phone lists · PC rows

On the phone, editing and deleting live behind a long-press that nothing on screen hints at. On both apps the whole row is a “complete” button, which is the most destructive target on the screen.

Fix

The checkbox completes. Tapping the row opens it. Phone: swipe right to complete, swipe left for 내일로 / 삭제. PC: row actions (✎ · 내일로 · ⋯) appear on hover.

4
The same number three times, and a giant clock P1
PC header

“1건 남음” appears in the title bar, in the ring and in the card header. A 42px clock copies the taskbar clock and takes the best spot on the screen, where the add bar should go.

Fix

One progress line: “오늘 5건 중 3건 남음” with a thin bar. The clock shrinks into the date line. The header space goes to the add bar and search.

5
Routines are the core, but hidden P1
PC home right column

“반복 업무” is folded into a bar at the bottom right. Above it, the fixed-height 마감 and 메모 cards sit mostly empty.

Fix

Content-sized sections: 다가오는 마감, then 반복 업무 (open, each with its next date), then 메모. Sections scroll together instead of each keeping its own empty box.

6
The routine rule is three dropdowns P1
PC form · phone editor

“N번째 영업일 ▾ · 1번째 ▾ · 매월 ▾” hides every option until you open each menu, and reads like a database. The preview, the best part, sits at the bottom, far from what changes it.

Fix

A sentence built from chips: 매월 · 마지막 · 영업일, with the next five dates right under it, plus a note when a date moved because of a weekend or holiday.

7
Settings you have to negate P1
forms · settings

“이 항목은 알림 띄우지 않기” (a checkbox that turns something off). “알림 (분 전)” is a number field. Settings mixes instant buttons (login, shortcut) with a Save button for the rest.

Fix

A positive switch “알림 받기” with chips 정각 · 10분 · 30분 · 1시간 · 하루 전. Settings apply as you change them, with no Save. The file path moves under “고급”.

8
The checkbox doesn't look like one P1
all lists

A very faint 18px circle, next to a colour bar whose meaning (마감 / 반복 / 메모) is never explained.

Fix

A 20px circle with a clear teal ring that fills on hover. The kind shows as a word (반복) only when it isn't obvious from the section.

9
Different words for the same thing P1
both apps

“마감이 있는 일” / “다가오는 마감” / “예정”; “반복되는 일” / “반복 업무” / “반복”; “기한 없는 메모” / “메모”.

Fix

할 일 (has a date), 반복, 메모. Views: 오늘 · 다가오는 일 · 반복 · 메모 · 달력.

10
No search, and item management is a tiny link P2
PC footer

“전체 항목 관리” is an 11px link in the bottom-left corner.

Fix

A search field in the header (Ctrl+K). Results open the editor. “전체 항목” becomes a view of search with no text.

11
Phone: the add button covers the list, and settings are missing P2
phone home

The floating “＋ 새 항목” pill sits over the last row. Reminder lead time and briefing time can only be changed on the PC. The account and reminder switch hide in “⋯”.

Fix

An add bar at the bottom, in thumb reach. A proper settings page: 알림 · 브리핑 · 영업일 · 계정 · 동기화 상태.

12
Small, faint text P2
both apps

Labels at 10–11px in the faintest colour, and 1px rules at 8.5% opacity.

Fix

Nothing under 12px. Secondary text at least 4.5:1 contrast. Rules stay subtle, but row spacing does the separating.

Today, for reference

Home: the count appears three times (4), routines are folded away (5), and the whole row is a complete button (3).
Add: the recurring tab is first, the rule uses three dropdowns (6), and the reminder is a negative checkbox (7).

Keep

The calm paper look, teal accent and Paperlogy type
Next-five-dates preview while building a rule
“This is not a business day → move to Friday?” warnings
Notification cards with 완료 · 10분 뒤 · 열기
Missed and held alerts folded into one card
Sync that just works, with its status shown in settings
